Initial F-Droid release.

- Unlock your LUKS-encrypted laptop remotely over the Syncthing relay
  network with a single biometric tap.
- Passphrase is held in EncryptedSharedPreferences, under a key kept in
  the Android Keystore.
- Replaced Google ML Kit with FLOSS ZXing for QR scanning.
- No tracking, no analytics, fully open source (AGPLv3+).
