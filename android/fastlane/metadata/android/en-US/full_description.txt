Syncthing LUKS Unlocker allows you to remotely unlock your Linux laptop's encrypted hard drive during the boot phase (initramfs). 

Using the Syncthing global relay network, it establishes a secure, NAT-traversing WebRTC connection to your laptop, retrieves your long LUKS passphrase from your phone's Android Secure Keystore, and transmits it instantly with a single biometric tap.

Features:
- No need to type long passphrases on your laptop keyboard
- Fully open-source (AGPLv3+)
- Uses Android Secure Hardware Keystore for encryption
- Connects globally without port forwarding (Syncthing Relays)
- Zero tracking or analytics
